About Wrenfield

A small dealership with one focus: family safety, done properly.

Wrenfield Motors is a Melbourne dealership built around a simple premise: most family cars treat safety as a checkbox, not a design principle. We work with one platform, in two body styles, so every engineering decision, from ISOFIX placement to rear door geometry, gets the attention a shared platform allows rather than being spread across a large lineup.

Both the Wrenfield SUV and Wrenfield Sedan carry a 5-star ANCAP safety rating, full ISOFIX and top-tether child seat anchoring on every rear seat, and an active-safety suite as standard, not an option pack. We sell to families first, and that shapes everything from our showroom hours to how our test drive booking works.
