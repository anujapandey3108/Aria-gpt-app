Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Wrenfield Motors ("Wrenfield," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at wrenfield.aiaccelerate.com.au and the associated test drive booking system, including access provided through AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. This policy explains what information we collect when you use these services and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a test drive booking, whether through our website or through an AI assistant connected to our booking system, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Your selected vehicle model and dealer location
  • Your preferred appointment date and time
  • Any optional notes you choose to include with your booking

We do not collect payment information, government identification, or other sensitive personal data as part of the booking process.

How we use this information

We use the information you provide solely to create, confirm, and manage your test drive appointment, and to contact you regarding that appointment if needed. We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing purposes without your separate consent.

Booking through AI assistants

Our booking system is accessible as a tool for AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. When you book a test drive through one of these assistants, the same information listed above is sent directly to our booking system to create your appointment. We do not receive your broader conversation history with the AI assistant, only the specific booking details you or the assistant provide to our system.

Data storage and retention

Booking information is stored to fulfil your appointment and for our internal records. If you would like your booking information deleted, contact us using the details on our support page.

Third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as required to operate our booking system (for example, our hosting providers) or where required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us through our support page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, visit our support page.
